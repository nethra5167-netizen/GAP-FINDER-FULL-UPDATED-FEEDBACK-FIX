How Automatic Gap Detection Works

Our evidence-based algorithm analyzes answers across repeated attempts.

Step 01

Diagnostic Test

Students answer diagnostic questions mapped to specific concepts and topics.

Step 02

Concept Analysis

Calculates cumulative accuracy across attempts. Requires evidence before flagging weakness.

Step 03

Clear Diagnostic Reasons

Explains precisely why the student struggled, pinpointing the misunderstanding.

Step 04

Actionable Revision

Delivers targeted practice questions and concepts to close the gap permanently.
